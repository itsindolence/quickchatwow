# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Up/Down in the input line browse your last 50 sent messages without holding Alt, kept between sessions
  (option, on by default; `/qchat arrows`).
- The channel colour bar on the input line shows only while typing (no stray bar on an idle input line).
- Flat thin scroll bar (no arrows or track), accent colour on hover; the "jump to newest" arrow is hidden.
- Tabs now sit in a header strip of the chat panel (drawn below the tabs), with a thin divider above the messages.
- Tabs stay bright when the mouse leaves the chat (no more fading to 20-40%); slightly taller header; docked tabs are sized to their labels for even spacing.
- The input line is aligned with the chat panel edges.

## 0.1.0
- First version: flat dark chat background with a thin border, flat tabs with an accent underline,
  channel-coloured input line, optional hiding of chat side buttons.
- Settings panel (Options > AddOns > QuickChat) and `/qchat` commands.
- English and Russian.
